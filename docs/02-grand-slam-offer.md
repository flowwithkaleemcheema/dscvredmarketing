> Built on the ICP in [`01-ideal-customer-profile.md`](./01-ideal-customer-profile.md), using Alex Hormozi's *$100M Offers* framework (value equation, problems → solutions, trim & stack, scarcity, urgency, bonuses, guarantee, MAGIC naming).
> **Avatar:** US owner-operated residential remodelers doing $1M–$5M a year; kitchen, bath and whole-home projects of $40k–$150k+.

## 2. Value equation

Hormozi's value equation: **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

| Lever | How the offer moves it |
|---|---|
| **Dream outcome ↑** | A calendar full of exclusive homeowners with $30k+ budgets, crews booked months out, and freedom from referral swings and Angi. Measured in **booked design consultations → signed projects**. |
| **Perceived likelihood ↑** | A specific numeric guarantee (20 in 90 days); a system built for remodelers only; transparent live ROI dashboard; one client per market (we can't hedge across competitors); proof from other industries; founding-partner terms that put the risk on us. |
| **Time delay ↓** | **Live in 14 days.** The dead-lead and past-estimate revival campaign produces booked consultations in **week 1**, before ads even launch. |
| **Effort & sacrifice ↓** | Done for you. The owner's only jobs: answer the alert, keep 4 consultation slots a week open, show up and close (which they're already good at). No content to create, no ad accounts to learn. |

## 6. The guarantee

### The Booked-Out Guarantee
> **"If you don't get 20 qualified design consultations within 90 days of your ads going live, we keep working at no management fee until you do."**

**Type:** Conditional "work for free until we hit it" (service guarantee). The setup fee is not refunded. Ad spend is always paid by the client.

**What counts as a qualified consultation** (agreed in writing at kickoff):
- A **homeowner**, with the decision maker attending
- Inside the client's agreed **service area**
- Wants a **project type the client does** (kitchen, bath, whole-home, etc.)
- **Stated budget** at or above the client's minimum (default **$30k+**)
- Plans to **start within 6 months**
- **Booked on the calendar** for an in-home or virtual design consultation

**Client conditions** (the guarantee applies only if the client):
1. Keeps ad spend at **$3,000/month or more** for the full 90 days (no pausing)
2. **Responds to new leads within 15 minutes** during business hours (the system alerts them), or lets the booking flow schedule directly
3. Keeps **at least 4 consultation slots a week** open on the booking calendar and **shows up** to booked appointments

**When the clock starts:** day 1 = the day ads go live, not the signing date.

**Copy version for the landing page:**
> *20 qualified consultations in 90 days, or we work for free until you get them. No tricks, no fine print beyond three things any serious remodeler already does: keep your ad budget running, call your leads back, and show up to your appointments.*

## 7. Scarcity and urgency

**Scarcity (all real, all enforceable):**
- **One remodeler per market.** We never run ads for two competitors in the same service area. Once a metro is taken, it's closed.
- **3 Founding Partner spots.** Setup fee waived in exchange for a video testimonial and case-study rights *after* the guarantee is met.
- **Onboarding capacity:** we onboard at most **2 new remodelers a month** so every launch hits the 14-day promise. *(Adjust to actual team capacity.)*

**Urgency:**
- **Seasonal:** remodel pipelines lag 60–120 days. Launching in Oct–Nov fills Jan–Mar, the slowest months of the year. "The time to fix your Q1 is now."
- **Founding Partner window** closes when 3 spots are filled.
- **Territory:** "Check if your market is still available" (a competitor may claim it first).

## 9. Front-end offer: the door opener

### Free Territory Check + Remodel Pipeline Audit
**Hook:** *"We work with one remodeler per market. Check if yours is still available."*

**Flow:**
1. **Cold email / LinkedIn / Facebook DM** → link to the landing page (or a direct reply)
2. **Territory Check form** (short, multi-step) → qualifies the prospect and confirms the market is open
3. **Book a 30-minute Pipeline Audit call.** We show them:
   - Monthly search demand for kitchen, bath and remodel terms in their service area
   - Which competitors are running ads and what they say (Google Ads Transparency Center, Meta Ad Library)
   - Their lead leaks: website, GBP, reviews, response time
   - A projected consultations → projects → revenue plan for their budget
4. **Present the Booked-Out Remodeler System** on the same call (or a short follow-up) → close

**Territory Check form fields** (these feed the landing page later):
1. Company name + website
2. Primary service area / metro
3. Main project types (kitchen / bath / whole-home / other)
4. Average project size (<$25k · $25–50k · $50–100k · $100k+)
5. Annual revenue (<$1M · $1–3M · $3–5M · $5M+)
6. Current lead sources (referrals / Angi-HomeAdvisor / Houzz / ads / other)
7. Monthly marketing budget available (<$3k · $3–5k · $5–10k · $10k+)
8. How many more projects a month you could take on (1 · 2–3 · 4+)
9. Name, email, mobile

**Auto-disqualify / route-out:** average project under $25k, or ad budget under $3k → polite "not a fit yet" page with a free resource.

## 14. Open items and risks to watch

1. **Delivery cost per client:** keep software (CRM, call tracking, dashboard) plus fulfilment within roughly 30–40% of the retainer.
2. **Google Local Services Ads** require business verification and background checks, which can take 1–3 weeks. Start this at kickoff, and don't make the 14-day launch depend on it.
3. **Guarantee exposure:** while working free, the client must keep paying ad spend. Track consultation counts weekly and fix course early, around day 30 and day 60.
4. **Founding-partner contract:** put the testimonial and case-study rights in writing.
5. **Stated values** are anchors. Replace them with real results after the first 3 partners.
6. **Localisation for AU/UK** (phase 2): currency, terms ("renovations", "extensions"), platforms (hipages, Checkatrade), and any advertising-standards wording for guarantees.
